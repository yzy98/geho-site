Before you begin, complete the [Quick Start](/docs) and keep the **Dashboard**, **API** and **Worker** running.

## Create an account and organization

Open the **Dashboard**, create an account, and create your organization.

<Callout title="Note">
  Your model providers, knowledge bases, and chatbots belong to this organization.
</Callout>

## Add model providers

Create two model provider configurations in the **Dashboard**:

- A **Chat** configuration for chatting LLM.
- An **Embedding** configuration for indexing knowledge and searching it.

Choose an available model for each capability and provide the credentials required by your provider.

## Create a knowledge base

Create a knowledge base named `Demo Knowledge Base` and select your embedding provider.

## Add sample knowledge

Open the knowledge base and add a text source.

Use `Shipping Policy` as its title and paste:

```text
Acme Store ships orders within 2 business days.

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days after dispatch.

Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery.

To request a return, email support@example.com with the order number.
```

Wait until the source status is `ready` before continuing.

If the source remains `pending`, check that the Worker and Redis are
running. If it becomes `failed`, inspect the reported error and check
your embedding provider configuration.

## Create a chatbot

Create a chatbot named `Demo Support`.

Select:

- Your chat model provider.
- `Demo Knowledge Base`.

For its system instructions, use:

```text
You are Acme Store's support assistant.
Answer clearly and concisely using the available store policies.
```

## Ask a question

Open the chatbot's preview and ask:

```text
How long does standard delivery take after dispatch?
```

The answer should state **3–5 business days** and include a citation to the sample knowledge. The exact wording may vary.

Check that the cited content supports the answer.
