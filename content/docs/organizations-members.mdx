## Organizations

An organization is Geho's workspace and tenant boundary. Model providers, knowledge bases, sources, chatbots, embed keys, and chat activity belong to one organization.

After signing up, create an organization during onboarding. The user who creates it becomes its owner.

Data from one organization cannot be selected or used by resources in another organization.

## Roles

| Role | Current product access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Can create Geho resources, create embed keys, and add members. |
| Member | Can view the organization's resources and members. |

The Dashboard hides creation actions when your role does not have the required permission.

## Add a member

Open **Members**, select **Add member**, and enter the person's email address.

The person must already have a registered Geho account. Adding an unknown email returns an error, and an existing member cannot be added twice.

Newly added users receive the member role.

## Current management capabilities

You can create an organization, list its members, and add registered users as members. Changing roles, removing members, inviting unregistered users, updating organizations, and deleting organizations are not available in the current Dashboard.
